Join on Console

Consoles can't type in a server address, so the free BedrockTogether phone app passes it to your console for you.

Server details

Address
AdventCraft.wdn.gg
Port
443

On Windows, iPhone, iPad or Android, you don't need the app. In Minecraft, open Play, then Servers, add a server and enter these details.

Before you start

  • Install BedrockTogether on your phone from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Update Minecraft on your console.
  • Put your phone and console on the same Wi-Fi network. It can't be a guest network, and your phone's mobile data should be off.
  • Turn off any VPN on your phone.

Xbox and PlayStation

  1. Open BedrockTogether and stay on the PlayStation · Xbox tab.
  2. Enter the address and port from above.
  3. Tap Watch Ad and Run. Keep the app open and your phone awake.
  4. On your console, open Minecraft, select Play, then the Worlds tab.
  5. Select the server. It appears as a LAN game.
  6. Once you've loaded in, you can close the app.

Nintendo Switch

Switch can't see LAN games, so the app broadcasts the server through a separate Xbox account. This method is still in beta.

Use a spare Microsoft account, never your main one. BedrockTogether warns that broadcasting may break Xbox's terms and the account could be banned.

  1. Create the spare Microsoft account and give it a gamertag at xbox.com.
  2. From your Switch, add that gamertag as a friend.
  3. In BedrockTogether, open the Switch · Friends tab and enter the address and port from above.
  4. Tap Sign in and Broadcast, read the warning, and sign in with the spare account.
  5. Tap Broadcast to Friends.
  6. On your Switch, open Minecraft, select Play, then the Friends tab, and join the spare account's game.
  7. When you've finished playing, tap Stop Broadcast.

If it doesn't work

  • The server isn't listed: check that your phone and console show the same Wi-Fi name, and keep the app open until you've joined.
  • On iPhone: allow Local Network access for BedrockTogether in Settings.
  • On Android: exclude the app from battery optimization.
  • Still not listed: your router may be blocking devices from seeing each other. Turn off AP isolation or use the Nintendo Switch method, which also works on other consoles.
  • Unable to connect to world: check the port is 443, then update Minecraft.

More fixes are in the BedrockTogether troubleshooting guide.
